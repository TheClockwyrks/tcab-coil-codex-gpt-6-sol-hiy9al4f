# Coil

A bright serpent cuts through a quiet grid. Keep it moving, guide each right-angle turn, and thread a safe path through the space your body leaves behind.

Every glowing pellet grows the coil and adds points. The walls and your own trail end the run at once, so each extra segment makes the next choice matter more.

Eat again before the combo bar runs dry to climb toward x5. A long route may be safe, but the best scores come from finding a quick route through a board that gets tighter with every bite.

Use arrow keys or WASD to steer. Press P or Escape to pause, and M to toggle sound. Classic is ready for another run as soon as the last one ends.
